- Colored header bar with title (36pt bold, white text)
- Thin accent line separator
- 3-5 bullet points per slide
- Clean 18pt text with perfect spacing
- Professional line height (1.2)
- Wide margins for readability
- Bullets are concise and impactful

## ✨ Quality Details

### Typography
- **Titles**: 36-54pt, bold, high contrast
- **Body text**: 18pt for optimal readability
- **Line spacing**: 1.2 for comfortable reading
- **Spacing**: 16pt between bullets

### Layout
- **Margins**: Generous (1 inch all sides)
- **Alignment**: Professional left-aligned bullets
- **Hierarchy**: Clear visual hierarchy
- **Balance**: Good text-to-white-space ratio

### Professional Polish
- Consistent styling across all slides
- Smooth color transitions
- Clear visual flow
- Easy to follow
- Not overcrowded
- Professional spacing

## 🎨 Customization Preview

### Can Add Professional Themes?

**YES! Unlimited themes in 2 minutes:**

1. Open `config.yaml`
2. Add your theme:

```yaml

  accent_color: [218, 165, 32]     # Your brand gold
```
